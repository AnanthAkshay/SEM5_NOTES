Course Outcomes(COs): 
At the end of the course, the student will be able to: 
1. Describe the need for machine learning, its relationship to other fields, and different types 
of machine learning. 
2. Analyse the fundamental principles of univariate, bivariate and multivariate data, 
dimensionality reduction techniques and learning system. 
3. Apply sim ilarity-based learning methods and perform linear, polynomial regression 
analysis 
4. Apply supervised learning techniques to classify datasets and make predictions. 
5. Analyse unsupervised learning techniques to cluster data and discover associations.
